# Autonomous workflows

1. Discover product. 2. Validate Canada eligibility. 3. Calculate supplier cost. 4. Apply 100% markup before shipping. 5. Add shipping. 6. Publish or queue approval. 7. Synchronize stock and price. 8. Receive paid-order webhook. 9. Revalidate. 10. Place supplier order through the live-order gate. 11. Synchronize tracking and fulfillment.
